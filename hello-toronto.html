Hello, Toronto! 🍁

We're going on a trip to Canada! Let's learn a few fun things about Canada and Toronto before we go — then spot them for real once we're there.

Meet Canada

🇨🇦

The Flag

Red and white, with a maple leaf right in the middle.

🦫

The Beaver

Canada's special animal! Beavers use their big teeth to build dams in rivers.

🍁

The Maple Leaf

Canada's favorite symbol. Sweet maple syrup comes from maple trees too!

🏒

Ice Hockey

Canada's favorite sport — played on ice skates with a puck and a stick.

🪙

The Loonie

Canada's dollar coin has a picture of a bird called a loon on it.

❄️

Snowy Winters

It gets cold and snowy — time for mittens, boots, and hot cocoa!

Toronto, Here We Come!

The CN Tower

One of the tallest towers in the world! You can even see it from far away.

🌊

Lake Ontario

A lake so big it looks like the ocean, right next to the city.

🚋

Streetcars

Trains that drive right on the street, like a bus on rails.

🏀

Raptors & Blue Jays

Toronto's basketball and baseball teams — maybe we'll spot a jersey!

🚇

The Subway

A train that zooms underground to get around the city fast.
